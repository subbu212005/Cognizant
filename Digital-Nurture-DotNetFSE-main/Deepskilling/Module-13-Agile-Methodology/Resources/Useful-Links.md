# Useful Links & Recommended Reading

A curated list of official guides, learning resources, books, and interactive tools to deepen your Agile and Scrum knowledge.

---

## Official Guides & Frameworks

* **[The Official Scrum Guide](https://scrumguides.org/)**: The definitive guide to Scrum, written by its creators Jeff Sutherland and Ken Schwaber.
* **[The Agile Manifesto](https://agilemanifesto.org/)**: The original manifesto website containing the values and principles.
* **[Scrum Alliance](https://www.scrumalliance.org/)**: Professional Scrum organization providing certification tracks (CSM, CSPO).
* **[Scrum.org](https://www.scrum.org/)**: Professional Scrum resource hub and assessment provider (PSM, PSPO).

---

## Recommended Books

1. **"Scrum: The Art of Doing Twice the Work in Half the Time"** by Jeff Sutherland.
   * *Why read*: Excellent introduction to the history, philosophy, and real-world impact of the Scrum framework.
2. **"Agile Estimating and Planning"** by Mike Cohn.
   * *Why read*: The definitive textbook on why relative estimation, story points, and planning poker are effective.
3. **"User Stories Applied: For Agile Software Development"** by Mike Cohn.
   * *Why read*: Detailed guide on how to write user stories, run user story workshops, and manage requirements.
4. **"User Story Mapping: Discover the Whole Story, Build the Right Product"** by Jeff Patton.
   * *Why read*: Explains how to visualize requirements sequentially to plan releases and Minimum Viable Products (MVPs).

## Online Estimation & Planning Tools

* **[Planitpoker.com](https://www.planitpoker.com/)**: Free online Planning Poker tool for distributed teams.
* **[Planningpoker.com](https://www.planningpoker.com/)**: Official Planning Poker tool powered by Mountain Goat Software.
* **[Miro Agile Templates](https://miro.com/templates/agile-workflows/)**: Interactive whiteboard templates for sprint retrospectives, story mapping, and daily standups.
* **[Figma FigJam Retrospectives](https://www.figma.com/templates/retrospective/)**: Quick-to-setup retrospective board templates.
