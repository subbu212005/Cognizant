# The Agile Manifesto

This section covers the core philosophical foundations of Agile software development. Drafted in 2001, the Agile Manifesto remains the definitive statement of agile values and principles.

---

## Section Contents

* **[Manifesto Notes](./Notes.md)**: Read the story behind the manifesto, the Snowbird meeting, and why it was created.
* **[Four Core Values](./Four-Values.md)**: Explore the four fundamental trade-offs defined by the manifesto.
* **[Twelve Principles](./Twelve-Principles.md)**: Learn about the twelve actionable principles that guide agile product teams.

---

## Learning Goals

In this section, you will learn:
* The historical context of the Snowbird, Utah meeting in 2001.
* How to interpret the "this over that" value structure.
* How to translate abstract principles into daily development habits.

1. Start by reading the historical background in **[Manifesto Notes](./Notes.md)**.
2. Study the **[Four Core Values](./Four-Values.md)** to understand the shifting priorities of agile teams.
