# Agile Estimation & Planning

Estimation and planning in Agile are continuous and collaborative. Instead of making big upfront plans, agile teams plan in concentric loops (the planning onion) and estimate work relatively to maintain speed and flexibility.

* **[Planning Notes](./Notes.md)**: Introduction to the "planning onion" and absolute vs. relative estimation.
* **[Story Points](./Story-Points.md)**: Learn what Story Points are, relative sizing, and the Fibonacci sequence.
* **[Planning Poker](./Planning-Poker.md)**: Step-by-step guide to conducting relative estimation sessions using poker cards.
* **[Sprint Planning](./Sprint-Planning.md)**: How to plan sprint backlog capacity and align on the Sprint Goal.
* **[Velocity](./Velocity.md)**: Learn how to calculate team velocity, use it for forecasting, and avoid common anti-patterns.

## Learning Goals

In this section, you will learn:
* The difference between strategic, release, and daily planning levels.
* Why relative estimation (points) is more accurate and less stressful than absolute estimation (hours).
* How to run a Planning Poker session and reach consensus.
* How to use team velocity and burndown charts to manage expectations and forecast releases.
