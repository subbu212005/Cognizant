# The Scrum Ceremonies (Events)

Scrum uses structured events to create regularity, optimize collaboration, and inspect progress. All events are **timeboxed**, meaning they have a maximum duration.

---

## Summary of Scrum Events (Based on a 4-Week Sprint)

| Event | Timebox | Primary Participants | Key Input(s) | Key Output(s) | Primary Goal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Sprint** | 1-4 Weeks | Entire Team | Product Backlog | Shippable Increment | Build a working product slice. |
| **Sprint Planning** | Max 8 Hours | Entire Team | Product Backlog, Capacity | Sprint Goal, Sprint Backlog | Plan the Sprint's scope & approach. |
| **Daily Scrum** | 15 Minutes | Developers | Current progress | Daily plan update | Inspect progress & adjust daily work. |
| **Sprint Review** | Max 4 Hours | Entire Team + Stakeholders | Increment, Backlog state | Updated Product Backlog | Demonstrate work & collect feedback. |
| **Sprint Retrospective** | Max 3 Hours | Entire Team | Process observations | Process improvement item(s) | Inspect team performance & adapt. |

*(Note: If Sprints are shorter, e.g., 2 weeks, the timeboxes are proportionally shorter: Planning ~4 hours, Review ~2 hours, Retrospective ~1.5 hours).*

---

## In-Depth Event Breakdowns

### 1. The Sprint
The Sprint is the container for all other events. It is a fixed-length event of one month or less to create consistency. A new Sprint starts immediately after the conclusion of the previous Sprint.
* **Rule**: No changes are made that would endanger the Sprint Goal. Quality goals do not decrease.

### 2. Sprint Planning
Sprint Planning initiates the Sprint by laying out the work to be performed. The entire Scrum Team collaborates to answer three questions:
1. **Why** is this Sprint valuable? (Result: **Sprint Goal**)
2. **What** can be delivered in this Sprint? (Result: **Backlog Selection**)
3. **How** will the chosen work get done? (Result: **Decomposed Tasks**)

### 3. Daily Scrum
A 15-minute event for the Developers to inspect progress toward the Sprint Goal and adapt the Sprint Backlog as necessary, adjusting the incoming planned work.
* **Rules**: 
  * Held at the same time and place every working day.
  * Facilitated *by* the Developers, *for* the Developers.
  * **Not a status report** to the Scrum Master or Product Owner, but an active planning session.
  * Common structure: What did I do yesterday? What will I do today? Are there any impediments?

### 4. Sprint Review
The purpose of the Sprint Review is to inspect the outcome of the Sprint and determine future adaptations. The Scrum Team presents the results of their work to key stakeholders, and progress toward the Product Goal is discussed.
* **Rule**: This is a collaborative working session, not just a presentation or slide show. The attendees inspect the working increment together.

### 5. Sprint Retrospective
The purpose of the Sprint Retrospective is to plan ways to increase quality and effectiveness. The Scrum Team inspects how the last Sprint went with regards to individuals, interactions, processes, tools, and their Definition of Done.
* **Rule**: The team identifies the most helpful changes to improve their effectiveness. The most impactful improvements are addressed as soon as possible (even added to the next Sprint Backlog).
